AI INTELLIGENCE LAYER

One AI intelligence layer. Multiple specialized capabilities.

VENDISYN uses transparent decision-support capabilities across the international patient journey. The system analyzes and organizes operational signals; authorized people remain responsible for patient communication and decisions.

01

AI Lead Analysis

Prepares which Lead to approach, why, and how before the sales team takes action.

Classifies every organic and paid Lead; evaluates messages, conversation history, private notes, patient intent and operational priorities together. It provides the sales team with explainable priorities and clear action strategies for every Lead.

02

AI Patient File Analysis

Turns fragmented information in the Patient File into a clear, action-ready roadmap for the sales team.

Continuously evaluates patient information, conversation records, Medical Review results, Pricing Request and Pricing Response records, and the Proposal process together throughout the patient journey. It presents current patient context, points requiring attention and recommended next actions.

03

AI Advertising Analysis

Measure advertising performance by real patients and verified sales—not only clicks and Lead volume.

Evaluates advertising channels, Campaign and Advertisement performance together with budget, Lead quality and verified sales. It measures CTR — Click-Through Rate, CPL — Cost per Lead and ROAS — Return on Advertising Spend; reveals the difference between high Lead volume and high Lead quality, and gives management explainable digital advertising recommendations.

04

AI Supervisor

Keeps every Lead, every action and every sales opportunity under continuous operational supervision.

Monitors the operational workflows of sales teams, Agents working in outbound call operations and regional sales representatives in International Patient Departments. It detects delayed actions, pending work, unattended Leads, capacity risks and sales opportunities that may be lost, showing authorized managers what needs intervention, in which process and why.

05

AI Agent

Gives every Sales Representative a personal operational assistant that continuously evaluates their workspace.

Continuously evaluates the Lead, Patient File, communication and pending-work records in the Sales Representative's workspace. It summarizes records, explains why work is prioritized and provides explainable recommendations about which task to focus on, which Lead needs attention and what the next action should be.

06

AI Robotic Calling

No Lead is forgotten simply because the sales team ran out of time.

Automatically calls high-volume Lead pools that exceed the call team's capacity, conducts initial qualification conversations and organizes meetings between interested patients and Sales Representatives. It periodically calls eligible Leads previously unreachable, unanswered or assessed as unqualified, identifies renewed interest and returns them to the team as qualified, action-ready sales opportunities.
