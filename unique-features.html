Seven distinctive capabilities

Direct solutions for the critical points of international patient operations.

Advertising investment, sales capacity, patient communication and management visibility come together on one operational backbone.

01

Automatic lead distribution

Leads from advertising and organic sources are automatically assigned to active sales representatives using the institution's equal-order or percentage-based rules. Manual routing is reduced while balanced distribution and a single Owner are preserved.

02

Preventing lead overload with Robotic AI

Leads waiting beyond a defined time or exceeding human capacity can enter a Robotic AI calling flow. Initial call outcomes help filter the pool so the sales team can focus on priority candidates ready to engage.

03

Recovering patients from dormant and unqualified leads

Eligible leads previously marked unreachable or unqualified are handled in a controlled recovery pool. Robotic AI calling identifies renewed interest and returns qualified opportunities to the sales team.

04

Clear advertising performance measurement

Each lead is followed from platform and advertisement identifiers through qualification, Patient File, sale and HBYS registration. Measurement is based on real operational and revenue outcomes—not clicks alone.

05

Clear, decision-ready management reports

Lead quality, first response time, team workload, call outcomes, conversion rates and advertising performance are combined in one view. Management acts on current, traceable results instead of scattered spreadsheets.

07

Location-independent, integrated communication

Phone and WhatsApp calls, plus WhatsApp, e-mail and SMS messaging, can be managed through VENDISYN on computers or mobile devices. Notes, outcomes and communication history stay attached to the relevant Lead or Patient File.

06

Protected patient phone and e-mail information

Call center and sales representatives can communicate without seeing the patient's real phone number or e-mail address. Details are masked by default and every action starts inside VENDISYN. Exceptional viewing access is granted only by an authorized administrator through role-based permissions.
